Navis for macOS bundles the libmpv (mpv) and FFmpeg libraries, licensed
under the GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL): mpv 0.36.0 under version
2.1 or later, and the FFmpeg 6.0 libraries (libavcodec, libavformat,
libavfilter, libavutil, libswscale, libswresample) under version 3 or later.
The full texts of both licences follow this notice.

These libraries are dynamically linked as separate frameworks inside the app
bundle (Navis.app/Contents/Frameworks) and can be replaced: Mpv.framework,
Avcodec.framework, Avformat.framework, Avfilter.framework, Avutil.framework,
Swscale.framework and Swresample.framework.

Upstream build
--------------

The frameworks are the unmodified binaries of the media-kit libmpv-darwin-build
release v0.6.0, flavour macos-universal-audio-default (arm64 and x86_64),
delivered through the media_kit_libs_macos_audio 1.1.4 Flutter package:

Release: https://github.com/media-kit/libmpv-darwin-build/releases/tag/v0.6.0
Archive: libmpv-xcframeworks_v0.6.0_macos-universal-audio-default.tar.gz
Source, patches and build scripts: https://github.com/media-kit/libmpv-darwin-build/tree/v0.6.0

That build is configured without GPL components (mpv with -Dgpl=false, FFmpeg
with --enable-version3 and without --enable-gpl). Its downloads.lock names the
exact upstream source archives it builds, and the patches applied to FFmpeg
and mpv are in the patches folder of the repository above:

FFmpeg 6.0: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-6.0.tar.xz
mpv 0.36.0: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/archive/refs/tags/v0.36.0.tar.gz
Mbed TLS 3.4.1: https://github.com/Mbed-TLS/mbedtls/archive/refs/tags/v3.4.1.tar.gz

For source assistance, write to Porthorion at support@porthorion.com.
